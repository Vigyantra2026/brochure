MILESTONE ANNIVERSARY

25 YEARS. ONE LEGACY.

A quarter century dedicated to cultivating intellectual rigor, transformative engineering, and uncompromising competitive spirit.

In 2001, our institution inaugurated an inter-college technical gathering aimed at testing the practical acumen of engineering students. Today, twenty-five years later, VIGYANTRA has evolved into a premier national technical symposium — a battleground where thousands of collegiate innovators assemble to tackle computing, robotic, algorithmic, and design challenges.

The 25th Silver Jubilee Edition stands as a testament to our relentless pursuit of technological relevance. It bridges the gap between academic textbooks and industrial reality, empowering students to challenge conventional boundaries, engineer resilient architectures, and discover solutions that redefine what is technically possible.
